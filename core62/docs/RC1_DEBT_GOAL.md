# Monthly debt and opening savings
Monthly outgoing debt projection allocates remaining principal by current-month payment and monthly amount (legacy month-first convention); no additional obligation or cash record. Horizon limits projected rows; later remaining principal stays in obligation. Future-dated settlements block historical projection. Goals with saved balance use an explicitly undated opening balance observed at migration asOf, not invented historic contributions. Earlier history marked unavailable. No actual cash movement. UI supports monthly debt plan and displays opening goal date. CI pending. No production migration.
